## What this measures

Every question below was put to the existing Ask Brain and the **behaviour** was checked: did retrieval find the right kind of source, was every claim cited, did confidence match the evidence, did the system refuse what it must refuse, and did it state anything the retrieved text does not contain.

**No golden answer is stored anywhere.** A question passes when the system handled it honestly - which includes answering "the retrieved evidence does not cover this". Whether an answer is *correct* is still a human's judgement, and nothing here replaces reading the cited documents.

## What to do next

- Everything passed. The useful next step is to grow the question set: add the questions people actually asked this week, especially the ones that came back Low confidence in `inspect_retrieval_quality.py`.
- Re-run this after any change to retrieval, the prompt, or the audit rules. That is the point of a fixed question set: it tells you whether a change helped or quietly hurt.

Evaluation is a smoke alarm, not a fire inspection. It proves the system is behaving as designed on a fixed set of questions. It cannot tell you whether an answer is right, and it never will - that is what the citations are for.
