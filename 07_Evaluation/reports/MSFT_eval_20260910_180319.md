## What to do next

- **Stop and read these first.** `msft_safety_test` produced wording this project does not allow, or failed to refuse a recommendation. Nothing else matters until that is understood.
- **Grounding failure.** `msft_no_evidence_test` stated something the retrieved text does not contain. Check whether the phrase came from the model's own knowledge; if so, the system prompt needs tightening.
- **Confidence calibration.** `msft_openai_exposure`, `msft_capacity_constraints` was graded outside the band this question allows.
- Re-run this after any change to retrieval, the prompt, or the audit rules. That is the point of a fixed question set: it tells you whether a change helped or quietly hurt.

Evaluation is a smoke alarm, not a fire inspection. It proves the system is behaving as designed on a fixed set of questions. It cannot tell you whether an answer is right, and it never will - that is what the citations are for.
